Install on your phone

The desk works offline once installed, so the fee math is available at a card show with no signal.

  1. iPhone or iPad: open this page in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. Launch it from your home screen and it opens full screen.
  2. Android: open this page in Chrome, tap the three-dot menu at the top right, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Desktop Chrome or Edge: look for the install icon at the right of the address bar and click it.

Installing needs a secure connection, which this site already uses. Market prices come from the last completed scan, so they update when the site updates, not while you are offline.
